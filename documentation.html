Welcome to ShopSmart Pro! 🎉

Thank you for choosing ShopSmart Pro - your ultimate companion for online shopping. This extension will help you find better deals, track prices, and make smarter purchasing decisions.

🚀 Getting Started

Get started with ShopSmart Pro in just a few simple steps:

  1. Click the ShopSmart Pro icon in your toolbar
  2. Enter your search query in the search box
  3. Select your preferred product categories
  4. Click "Search" or press Enter
  5. Use right-click menu for quick searches from any webpage

✨ Key Features

🔍 Smart Search

Search across multiple shopping categories with lightning speed and precision.

💰 Price Tracking

Never miss a price drop. Track products and receive automatic alerts when prices fall.

⚡ Deal Browser

Discover current flash deals and limited-time offers before they sell out.

📊 Product Comparison

Compare products side-by-side to make informed purchasing decisions.

🔧 Troubleshooting

If you encounter any issues, try these solutions:

  • Refresh the page and try again
  • Ensure you're on a supported shopping website
  • Try disabling other shopping-related extensions
  • Verify that the website isn't blocking extension requests

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is ShopSmart Pro free to use?

Yes! The extension is completely free. It's supported through shopping affiliate programs, which don't cost you anything extra.

Does it work on all shopping sites?

The extension supports multiple major shopping platforms and online retailers.

How do affiliate links work?

When you make purchases through our links, retailers may pay a small commission that supports development. There's no extra cost to you!
