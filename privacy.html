Privacy Policy for ShopSmart Pro

Last Updated: April 1, 2025

ShopSmart Pro ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

1. No Data Collection or Transmission

ShopSmart Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data stays on your device.

2. Local-Only Data Storage

The extension uses your browser's chrome.storage.sync to store:

  • Search history
  • Tracked products
  • Settings (country, category, dark mode)

This data syncs across your devices via Chrome Sync — just like bookmarks.

3. Permissions Explained

We request these permissions to function:

  • Web history: To detect when you're on Amazon or eBay.
  • User activity: To respond to clicks (e.g., "Track Price").
  • Storage: To save your preferences locally.

4. No Tracking, No Ads, No Selling

We do not:

  • Sell or share your data
  • Track your browsing
  • Display ads
  • Use your data for profiling

5. Third-Party Services

The extension interacts only with:

  • Amazon (product pages, search)
  • eBay (search)
  • Chrome/Firefox APIs (storage, tabs, notifications)

No data is sent to any server unless you visit a site directly.
